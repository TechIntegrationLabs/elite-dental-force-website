Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Elite Dental Force, the EDiFi platform, security, implementation, and pricing.

info General

Elite Dental Force is a dental revenue intelligence platform that combines AI-powered consultants with the EDiFi software platform. We help practices optimize eligibility verification, reduce claim denials, and improve overall revenue performance through real-time data analytics and intelligent automation.
Unlike traditional billing services, we combine human-guided AI consultants with our proprietary EDiFi software platform. This dual-layer approach provides both the strategic expertise of experienced dental billing professionals and the speed and accuracy of purpose-built technology, delivering results that neither humans nor software can achieve alone.
Elite Dental Force serves practices of all sizes, from single-location private practices to multi-state DSOs with hundreds of locations. Our platform scales seamlessly to meet the needs of any organization, with pricing and features tailored to your specific practice size and complexity.

dashboard Platform & Features

AI Consultants are our team of experienced dental billing professionals enhanced by artificial intelligence tools. They provide strategic guidance, complex case resolution, and personalized support. EDiFi is our software platform that handles real-time eligibility verification, claims tracking, revenue analytics, and workflow automation. Together, they form a complete revenue intelligence ecosystem.
EDiFi integrates with leading practice management systems including Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, Curve, Denticon, and many others. We also connect directly with major clearinghouses and payers for real-time eligibility verification and claims status updates. Our open API allows custom integrations with your existing technology stack.
Practices using Elite Dental Force typically see a 35% reduction in claim denials, 90%+ first-pass acceptance rates, and an average revenue recovery of $42,000+ within the first 90 days. Results vary based on practice size, current denial rates, and level of platform adoption.
EDiFi is a fully responsive web-based platform that works seamlessly on any device, including smartphones and tablets. You can access real-time dashboards, eligibility verification, and key metrics from anywhere. A dedicated mobile application with push notifications and offline capabilities is on our product roadmap.

shield Security & Compliance

We implement comprehensive security measures including AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, role-based access controls (RBAC), multi-factor authentication (MFA), and continuous monitoring. All data is stored in SOC 2 Type II certified data centers with redundant backups.
Yes. We provide a comprehensive BAA to all clients as part of our standard onboarding process. Our BAA covers data handling, breach notification procedures, permitted uses and disclosures, and subcontractor obligations. Contact our team to request a copy for review.
All data is stored in SOC 2 Type II certified data centers located within the United States. We maintain redundant backups across geographically distributed facilities to ensure high availability and disaster recovery capabilities. Data never leaves US-based infrastructure.

build Implementation & Support

Getting started is simple. Book a demo through our website, and our team will walk you through the platform, assess your practice needs, and create a custom implementation plan. From first contact to go-live typically takes 2-4 weeks depending on your practice size and integration requirements.
Our implementation follows a structured 4-phase approach: Discovery (practice assessment and goal setting), Integration (PMS connection and data migration), Training (staff onboarding and workflow configuration), and Launch (go-live with dedicated support). A dedicated implementation manager guides you through every step.
We provide comprehensive training including live onboarding sessions for your team, role-specific training modules, on-demand video tutorials, and detailed documentation. Ongoing training is available through our webinar series, and your dedicated AI Consultant provides continuous guidance and support.
All clients receive dedicated support through their assigned AI Consultant, priority email support, and access to our knowledge base. Premium support tiers include phone support, guaranteed response times, and on-site training. Our support team is available Monday through Friday, 8am-6pm in all US time zones.

payments Pricing

Our pricing is customized based on your practice size, number of providers, claims volume, and desired service level. We offer flexible plans that scale with your practice, and most clients see a positive ROI within the first 90 days. Contact our sales team for a personalized quote.
We offer both monthly and annual plans. Annual plans come with a discount, but we do not require long-term commitments. We're confident that once you see the results, you'll want to stay. All plans include a satisfaction guarantee during the initial implementation period.
